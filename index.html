        Agency charter
        
      Why one agency instead of eight separate fixes
      
      
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
        Articles I–III
        
      One standard for every property
      
        
      Every website in the portfolio gets the same checks, the same legal notice discipline, the same domain and inbox care. A fix that works on one property is applied to all of them.
      
      
      
      
      
        Articles IV–VI
        
      Permits come from the authority
      
        
      The agency prepares, files and tracks. Only the issuing authority grants a permit, license or registration, and every request names that authority. Nothing is shown as approved until it is.
      
      
      
      
      
        Articles VII–IX
        
      Concept first, claims second
      
        
      No property says it is doing something it is not yet doing. Until a product is tested, a license issued or a venue signed, the copy says "planned" or "proposed". Critical fixes are cleared before new building starts.
